> `Chaturaji Score Sheet.xlsx` (which remains in the repository's history). The workbook repeats
> each sheet below to fill a printed page: the score grid five times, the reference card twice.
> Where it and [rules.md](../rules.md) differ, rules.md is authoritative.

## Reference card

### Actions

- Promote pawn which made it to far end to any captured piece.
- Make ransom offer (exchange any number of captured pieces).
- Move one piece (not available if throne occupied).
- Roll two dice to potentially move two pieces.
  - If doubles are rolled but the piece was captured, it escapes!
  - If occupied, occupier takes second movement (if possible).
- If you are an occupier or a regent, you can also move the other players pieces on your turn.

### Dice

| Die value | Piece Moved |
|---|---|
| 1 | Boat |
| 2 | Horse (knight) |
| 3 or 4 | Elephant |
| 5 or 6 | Pawn or Raja (king) |

Rolled double can be used for captured piece to escape!
