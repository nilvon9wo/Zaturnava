# Open questions

Every question waiting on the designer's answer, and only those: there are no others in the other documents.
When one is answered, the answer goes where it belongs (the rules, the specification, the roadmap, the code),
marked *Stated* there, and the question here is cut down to whatever is still open, or removed.

Choices marked *Proposed* elsewhere are decisions made without an answer; they stand as they are and need none.
They are marked only so that it is known which choices were the designer's and which were not, should one ever
need to change. None is a question in disguise: a choice that cannot stand without the designer's answer is
asked here instead (checked, every *Proposed* in the documents, on 2026-11-06).
Anything to be built is in the [backlog](backlog.md) or the [roadmap](grand/roadmap.md), and anything left to
playtesting is marked so where it is written.

The player out of a game when their leaders are gone is being thought over by the designer, and waits in the
[backlog](backlog.md).

## Q-L. What does each movement lift let a piece do? (asked 2026-11-26)

Area lifts can be bought in the draft now ([roadmap, Stage 5](grand/roadmap.md#stage-5--points-to-spend)): a
palace's confinement or a river's, whose meaning is plain. A **movement** lift needs a stated move without its
restriction, and the catalogue gives one only for the Horse, the Knightrider and the Soldiers. Proposed, each to be
confirmed or corrected:

| Piece | Restriction | Lift | Proposed move once lifted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horse (Xiangqi) | The first square of its leg must be empty | 2 | Leaps as the Knight (*Stated*) |
| Knightrider | Each landing short of the last must be empty | 3 | Leaps on past an occupied landing (*Stated*) |
| Soldier (Xiangqi), Soldier (Janggi) | Never back | 2 | Also steps straight back (*Stated*) |
| Elephant (Janggi) | Each cell on the way must be empty | 2 | Leaps 3+2 freely, as the Zebra |
| Elephant (Xiangqi) | The square between must be empty | 2 | Leaps 2+2 freely, as the Alfil |
| Pawn (Western), Pawn (Makruk) | Blocked straight ahead | 2 | Also takes the piece straight ahead |
| Pawn (Berolina) | Blocked diagonally ahead | 2 | Also takes diagonally ahead |
| Lance (Shogi) | Blocked by pieces in its path | 2 | Slides forward through its own army's pieces, stopping on or before an enemy |
| Cannon (Janggi) | Must hop exactly one piece, and never a Cannon | 3 | May hop a Cannon, and take one |
| Grasshopper | Must hop, landing just beyond | 3 | Also steps one square any way, as the King, without hopping |

And the Janggi Soldier's *area* restriction (diagonally only along palace lines, lift 2): once lifted, also steps
diagonally forward anywhere, as the Silver General's forward diagonals.
