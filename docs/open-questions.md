# Open questions

Every question waiting on the designer's answer, and only those: there are no others in the other documents.
When one is answered, the answer goes where it belongs (the rules, the specification, the roadmap, the code),
marked *Stated* there, and the question here is cut down to whatever is still open, or removed.

Choices marked *Proposed* elsewhere are decisions made without an answer; they stand as they are and need none.
They are marked only so that it is known which choices were the designer's and which were not, should one ever
need to change. None is a question in disguise: a choice that cannot stand without the designer's answer is
asked here instead (checked, every *Proposed* in the documents, on 2026-11-06).
Anything to be built is in the [backlog](backlog.md) or the [roadmap](grand/roadmap.md), and anything left to
playtesting is marked so where it is written.

- **Pieces the families' games have that the catalogue lacks** (2026-11-20). The family bots' armies are now of
  their own game's pieces only. Pieces that move just as one already in the catalogue count as both games'
  (Makruk's Khun, Met, Ma and Ruea are the Western King, the Courtier, the Knight and the Rook; Xiangqi's and
  Janggi's Chariots the Rook, Janggi's Horse Xiangqi's; Shogi's King, Rook and Bishop the Western ones). One is
  missing outright: Shogi's Knight (Keima), which leaps only to the two squares a Knight's move straight ahead,
  never back. Should it be added, and at what price?
- **Checkers' and Othello's armies are tiny** (2026-11-20). Dealt at the same budget as everyone else, a Checkers
  army is a King and two Men (3 pieces: a Man costs 56, a Checkers King 67) and an Othello army 4 Evangelists (70
  each), against 18 pieces for a Makruk army on the large board. Is that as intended, the price of taking several
  at once, or should the Man and the Evangelist cost less, or such armies spend more?
